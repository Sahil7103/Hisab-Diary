Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Sahil Koshti, operating under the name Trevios, provides Hisab Diary, an app for keeping household delivery records and calculating bills. This policy describes the app's current data practices. It also describes this privacy-policy website where relevant.

2. Records stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter, including vendor names and service categories, daily quantities and rates, delivery schedules, attendance marks, monthly rate records, payment status, language preferences and reminder settings. A payment mark records your own payment status; the app does not transfer money or collect bank or card details.

We do not operate a server that stores or synchronizes your diary records. The app does not require a name, email address or phone number to use its core features. Archiving a vendor keeps its historical records on your device.

3. Analytics, crash reports and performance

Release versions enable Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring. These help us understand app usage, identify failures and improve reliability. Monitoring is disabled by default in development builds and may be disabled in a particular build.

  • Usage analytics: app interactions and screen views, sessions, app-instance identifiers, device and app information, language and approximate location derived by Google from technical information. Automatically collected events and identifiers depend on the Firebase SDK and device settings.
  • Crash reporting: crash and error information, stack traces, diagnostic logs, app/device information and installation identifiers.
  • Performance monitoring: startup and operation timings, technical device/network information and installation identifiers. SDK network measurements may include URLs, response status and timing information.

Our custom monitoring does not intentionally include vendor names, diary IDs, bill contents, quantities, rates, purchase tokens or raw exception messages. Firebase's automatic diagnostics are separate from our custom events. Technical monitoring data may be processed on Google's infrastructure outside your country.

Read Firebase's privacy and security information and Google's Privacy Policy for details of their collection, processing and safeguards.

4. Sharing bills and backups

Sharing happens when you choose it. The app prepares a bill or a backup and opens the device's share interface. The app or recipient you choose, such as WhatsApp, then receives that content under its own privacy practices.

Exported backups contain your diary records and selected settings. They are not encrypted by Hisab Diary. Store them securely and share them only with people or services you trust. Restoring a backup replaces the diary records on this phone after confirmation. Copies you shared or exported remain outside the app until you delete them separately.

5. Permissions and other services

Notification permission is used for optional reminders. Exact-alarm access, where available, is used for reminder timing. Reminder schedules and actions are handled locally. Internet access supports Firebase monitoring; core diary operations remain available offline. File selection and sharing use your device's system interfaces.

The current version is free and Pro purchases are disabled. It does not display advertisements. Google Play services or SDKs can have their own data practices; we do not sell your diary information.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local diary records remain in app storage until you clear the app's storage, replace them through a backup restore, or remove the app. To delete local records, use Android Settings → Apps → Hisab Diary → Storage → Clear storage. Menu names vary by phone. Removing app data does not delete exported files or copies held by other people or services. Device or operating-system backup and restore may also be governed by your Android settings.

Monitoring data already sent to Google is separate from local diary records; clearing app storage does not immediately delete that data. Analytics retention depends on the project's Google Analytics settings and Google's applicable policies. Firebase documents retention of crash data and associated identifiers for 90 days, and performance data for periods of 30 or 60 days depending on the data type, before beginning removal. See the linked Firebase documentation for current details.

You can turn off reminders in the app or revoke notification permissions in Android settings. The current app does not provide a user-facing switch to disable Firebase monitoring. Contact Trevios about privacy concerns or applicable access/deletion requests. We cannot retrieve diary records stored only on your device, and identifying a particular pseudonymous monitoring record may not be possible without additional information.

7. Security and disclosure

Diary records use the app's local storage within Android's application environment. Protect your device and exported backups. Firebase uses HTTPS for data sent to its services. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Google processes monitoring information to provide the services described above under its applicable terms. Information may also be disclosed when legally required. If you contact us, we use information you voluntarily provide to respond and handle your request; please send only what is necessary.

8. Children

Hisab Diary is intended for household record keeping and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can address the concern.
