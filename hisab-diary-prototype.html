हिसाब डायरी · Hisab Diary

Clickable prototype for the Flutter build. Seven screens shown in iPhone frames (the real app ships on Android). Tap the buttons inside the phones: marking a vendor, “सब आया”, calendar days, steppers and switches all respond.

  • Every button is at least 64px tall
  • Hindi first, English underneath, icon on every item
  • No login, no typing needed for daily use
  • Mark a day in 1 tap · send a bill in 2 taps · add a vendor in 3 taps
